Experts

Unchecking an expert removes them from consolidation, consensus and all indicators. Weights are used in the weighted geometric mean of judgments (default 1). Individual CR per group is RGMM-based (λ estimated from the row-geometric-mean weights, Alonso–Lamata formula), identical to the Excel input sheets and to the CR stored by the survey. The group cards show the eigenvalue-based CR (λmax of the principal eigenvector), identical to the Excel Summary sheet; for inconsistent matrices the two differ, the EVM value usually being slightly higher.

Global weights

Global weight = main-criterion weight × local sub-criterion weight. Weights are EVM (principal eigenvector, power method) on the scale-transformed consolidated matrix, exactly as in the Excel Summary sheet.
