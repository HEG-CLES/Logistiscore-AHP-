ℹ How to use this form

  1. Fill in your name and today's date.
  2. Read the Saaty scale reference before starting.
  3. For each pair, select which criterion is more important for urban last-mile deliverability, then choose the intensity (1–9).
  4. The Consistency Ratio (CR) is calculated automatically. If CR > 0.10, highlighted rows indicate the most inconsistent judgements — please revise them.
  5. When finished, click Copy export data and send the copied text by email to yvonne.badulescu@hesge.ch.

3Traffic sub-criteria being compared

C1Congestion
Ratio of free-flow speed to observed average speed on the segment. A low ratio indicates heavy congestion affecting delivery efficiency.
C2Roadworks
Presence and duration of active construction or incident works on the segment. Longer works create greater disruption to freight movements.
C3Average Speed
Observed average travel speed on the segment. Reflects actual traffic conditions and travel time efficiency for delivery vehicles.
C4Number of Lanes
Total number of traffic lanes available. More lanes generally improve circulation for trucks and vans; fewer lanes favour cargo bikes.
C5Intersection Type
Type of traffic control at the segment's intersection: priority road, traffic lights, roundabout, yield, or stop sign. Affects dwell time and flow.
C6Public Transport Stop
Presence and service frequency of nearby PT stops. Buses and trams stopping and re-entering traffic disrupt freight vehicle flow.
